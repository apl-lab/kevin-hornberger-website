Republican members of the House Ways and Means Committee today questioned [**how a non-citizen was able to register to vote and remain active on Maryland’s voter rolls**](https://www.wmar2news.com/infocus/maryland-republican-lawmakers-demand-review-of-voter-rolls-after-former-baltimore-principal-is-arrested-by-ice) year after he moved out of the state. In a letter to the Elections Administrator, Jared DeMarinis, members called for hearings on the matter to ensure confidence in Maryland’s elections before the 2026 election cycle. This inquiry follows a [**September 26 correspondence**](https://www.wmar2news.com/infocus/maryland-republican-lawmakers-demand-review-of-voter-rolls-after-former-baltimore-principal-is-arrested-by-ice) where House Republicans urged the Board of Elections to cooperate with inquiries from the U.S. Department of Justice [**regarding the state’s voter rolls**](https://foxbaltimore.com/news/local/watchdog-ian-roberts-maryland-voter-registration-application-red-flags). In the letter, members reiterated their support for the federal inquiry and suggested that the Department of Justice may be better placed to investigate instances of non-citizens registering to vote in Maryland.
