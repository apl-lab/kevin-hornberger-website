I am also proud to be endorsed by the Maryland House Republican Caucus. My Republican colleagues know my record because they have worked alongside me. They know I have consistently fought against tax increases, defended constitutional freedoms, supported law enforcement and first responders, and stood up to the liberal agenda coming out of Annapolis.

![](/images/news/1000003747.png)

Law enforcement is among the most important jobs in Cecil County, Maryland, and the United States. A civil and safe society thrives when law enforcement is effective and trusted - these are our neighbors too. Along with the help and support of several local first responders, I successfully passed 2019 HB-387 to extend tax benefits to law enforcement working in some of the most dangerous and uncertain conditions imaginable while patrolling transportation infrastructure, some of which are along highways and roads in Cecil County. 

Police have gotten a bad deal from Annapolis, most notoriously through the passage of 2021 HB-670. I voted NO. I have also introduced HB-508, which removes frivolous unfounded complaints against law enforcement officers from their file after a three-year statute of limitation.  I have been able to garner bipartisan support for this legislation, and plan to revisit the bill again next year.  This protects good police officers from retaliation or bad-faith complaints.

![](/images/news/1000003651-1-.png)

I have consistently voted to support the individual right to bear arms. I believe the Second Amendment is quite clear when it says “shall not be infringed”. I voted NO on Red Flag Laws, and support repealing the Firearm “Safety” Act of 2013 which makes lawful ownership of a handgun for the average citizen far too difficult, and arbitrarily restricts many long guns. I also opposed the recent arbitrary and unconstitutional ban on Glocks.

I am proud to stand with Cecil County firefighters and EMS.  Whether it is supporting our first responders via legislation such as 2026 HB-1602/SB-579 and 2022 HB-44/SB-295, or delivering capital funding for local fire departments, I am eager to continue my advocacy for the men and women dedicating themselves to this essential and venerable community service.

Kevin Hornberger Proposes Rolling Back MVA Fees

When Governor Wes Moore and Annapolis Democrats pushed higher costs onto Maryland families, I fought back. I sponsored amendments to roll back vehicle registration fee increases and worked to stop more money from coming out of your pocket.

Kevin Hornberger Proposes Gas Tax Holiday



With Maryland's automatic gas tax increase set to take effect next month, I proposed a gas tax holiday to provide immediate relief to working families, commuters, seniors, and small businesses already struggling with rising costs. Unfortunately, Annapolis Democrats rejected that effort. I will continue fighting against higher taxes and fees every step of the way.

SAVE Act Hearing, GLE Committee

I was proud to serve as the lead sponsor in Maryland of President Donald Trump's SAVE Act, legislation designed to strengthen election integrity and ensure that only American citizens vote in American elections. Secure elections are the foundation of our republic, and I'll never stop fighting to protect them.

Maryland Right to Life PAC has endorsed my re-election to the House of Delegates, District 35B. Whether it is stopping physician assisted suicide, fighting neo-eugenics legislation, or resisting the radical enshrinement of abortion, I look forward to continuing my advocacy for the most vulnerable.

Throwing more money at schools will not fix the systemic problems they face - accountability will.  I am a proponent of school choice, support the BOOST Scholarship Program, and believe that parents - not State bureaucracy - should be in control of their children's education.

I also understand 4-year bachelor degree programs are not for everybody.   Working with blue-collar education advocates, I passed greater access to already-existing scholarship funds for tradespeople, truck drivers, and everyday working citizens.
