[Thank you to the Tire Industry Association and its members for this recognition. I was honored to receive the 2025 Friend of the Industry Award](https://www.tireindustry.org/resources/resources/press-releases/2025/tia-announces-2025-hall-of-fame-inductees-friend-of-the-industry-award/).  Cecil County is home to numerous auto & tire businesses who employ a substantial number of constituents. Whether it is right-to-repair, use of rubber-modified asphalt, or any other public issue affecting the automotive industry, I am looking forward to continuing my legislative advocacy in 2026 and beyond.
