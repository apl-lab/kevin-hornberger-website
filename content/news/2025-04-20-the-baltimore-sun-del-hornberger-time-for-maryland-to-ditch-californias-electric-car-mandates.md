The Baltimore Sun, April 20th, 2025:

“Voters nationwide have made their voices heard loud and clear — the time for government mandates around how Americans spend their time and live their lives is over. For countless Americans and Marylanders, that includes choosing a car that fits their needs, not California’s. That means no more electric vehicle mandates in the Free State.”
